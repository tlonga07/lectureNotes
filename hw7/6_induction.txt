Use mathematical induction to prove that if n is natural number, then:

    1*1! + 2*2! + ... + n*n! = (n+1)! - 1

Base case/Prove n = 1:

LHS = 1*1! = 1

RHS = (1+1)! - 1 = 2! - 1 = 2 - 1 = 1

1 = 1

Base case good! P(1) holds.

Inductive step:

Assume P(k) holds for k.

11! + 22! + ... + k*k! = (k+1)! - 1

We want to prove P(k+1):

11! + 22! + ... + kk! + (k+1)(k+1)! = (k+2)! - 1

LHS P(k+1)

11! + 22! + ... + kk! + (k+1)(k+1)!

= (11! + 22! + ... + kk!) + (k+1)(k+1)!

= (k+1)! - 1 + (k+1)*(k+1)! (by inductive hypothesis)

= (k+1)! + (k+1)*(k+1)! - 1

= (k+1)!(1 + k + 1) - 1

= (k+1)!(k+2) - 1

= (k+2)! - 1 (since (k+2)! = (k+2)*(k+1)!)

= ((k+1)+1)! - 1

Proved P(k+1)

Since P(1) holds and P(k) implies P(k+1), the formula holds for all positive natural numbers n.
