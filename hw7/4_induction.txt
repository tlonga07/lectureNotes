
Use mathematical induction to prove that the sum of the first n even natural numbers equals n(n+1).
That is, that:

    2 + 4 + ... + 2n = n(n+1)
    
    if n = 3 both sides true

(Note that we are considering "2" to be the first even natural number.)

P(n) is:  2 + 4 + ... + 2n = n(n+1)
 
Base case/Prove n = 1: 
LHS = 2 & RHS = 1(1+1) = 2
2 = 2 
Base case good! P(1) Holds

Inductive step: 
Assume P(k) holds for k 
2 + 4 + ... + 2k = k(k+1)

LHS P(k+1)
2+4+6+...+2k +2(k+1)
= (2 + 4 + 6 + ... + 2k) + 2(k+ 1)
= k(k+1) + 2(k+1)
= (k+1)(k+2) 
Prove P(k + 1)
