Suppose we have these propositional atoms:

    p: It is below freezing.
    q: It is snowing.
    r: y <= 4
    s: x == 2

How would we express these as English sentences/conditions?

    ¬p: It is not below freesing

    p ∧ q: It is below freezing and it is snowing

    p → q : The fact it is below freezing implies that it is snowing. 

    ¬p → ¬q: If it isnt below freezing that implies it is not snowing

    r ∨ s: (y is smaller than or equal to four) OR (x is equal to 2)

    ¬r: y is not smaller than or equal to 4 // y is greater than for
