Use a direct proof to prove the following proposition:

The product of two odd integers is odd.

Proof: 
Assume both x and y are integers and odd. 

x = 2k + 1 for some k because 2 times any integer is even, so adding 1 will make it odd

same applies to y for some m

xy = (2k + 1)(2m + 1)
xy = 4km + 2k + 2m + 1
xy = 2(2km + k + m) + 1
xy = 2(whatever) + 1 when whatever = 2km+k+m


xy must also be odd as 2(whatever) is even, and adding 1 makes it odd.
