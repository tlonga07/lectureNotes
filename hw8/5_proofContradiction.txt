Use proof by contradiction to prove the following proposition:

The sum of an irrational and a rational number is irrational.

P = x is irrational and y is rational
Q = x + y is irrational

Contridiction:
Assume x + y is rational and x is irrational and y is rational

a/b when b is a non zero integer is a rational number

x + y = a/b 
y is rational so y = c/d  where d is a non zero int

x = a/b - y 
= a/b - c/d 
= ad - bc/bd  

Since a,b,c,d are all int, ad - bc and bd are also integers. bd != 0 since b and d are nonzero.
This would make x rational, but x can't be rational by our assumption, making a contridiciton.
x + y being rational must be false, so x + y is irrational.
